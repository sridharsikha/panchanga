Gītā Jayantī: when the Gītā was made known
  • Bhīṣma falls on Day 10. Sañjaya returns to Dhṛtarāṣṭra, opens with "hato bhīṣmaḥ" (6.14.1–3, "daśarātram" 6.14.11), then narrates the war from the start, including the Gītā (6.23–40).
  • Day 10 = Wednesday, 29 Oct 3138 BCE (Julian) · Mārgaśīrṣa Śukla Ekādaśī · Aśvinī. The Ekādaśī ran from about 8 pm on 28 Oct to 8 pm on 29 Oct, local time.
  • So: the Gītā was spoken on Śukla Pratipadā (Day 1) and revealed to the world on Mokṣadā Ekādaśī (Day 10). The text does not say how long Sañjaya's narration took.

Kali anchor from Śrīpāda Śrīvallabha Charitāmṛtam; the computed sky reproduces its weekday, tithi and nakshatra. The day before (19 Oct, Amāvāsyā) is the eve of battle referred to in 6.3.28. North-cycle names use the Sūrya Siddhānta Jupiter rule. The 36-year interval comes from 11.25.41 and 16.1.1.

6.3.28–33 (Vyāsa, just before battle)"imāṃ … amāvāsyāṃ trayodaśīm": this Amāvāsyā fell on the 13th day; Kṛṣṇa Caturdaśī had just passed; at today's dawn the sun was struck. So an Amāvāsyā falls on the eve of battle.
13.153.26–28 tithi arithmeticDay 1 on Śukla Pratipadā puts Day 10 on Mārgaśīrṣa Śukla 11. Adding 58 nights (about 59 tithis) gives Māgha Śukla 10, a third of the fortnight remaining, which matches the text. An Ekādaśī Day 1 gives Māgha Kṛṣṇa 3 instead, the wrong fortnight. (This assumes no intercalary month.)
9.33.5 vs 9.34.12Balarāma departs under Puṣya (9.33.5) but under Maitra/Anurādhā (9.34.12), so his own account is inconsistent.

Key verses

Udyoga 5.81.7
kaumude māsi revatyāṃ śaradante himāgame / sphītasasyasukhe kāle…
In the Kaumuda month, under Revatī, at autumn's end as winter came, in a season of plentiful harvest… (Kṛṣṇa sets out)
Udyoga 5.140.18
saptamāc cāpi divasād amāvāsyā bhaviṣyati / saṃgrāmaṃ yojayet tatra tāṃ hy āhuḥ śakradevatām
On the seventh day from now there will be a new moon. Let battle be joined then; they say Indra presides over it.
Bhīṣma 6.17.2
maghāviṣayagaḥ somas tad dinaṃ pratyapadyata / dīpyamānāś ca saṃpetur divi sapta mahāgrahāḥ
That day the Moon entered Maghā's domain; seven great planets blazed together in the sky.
Śalya 9.33.5
catvāriṃśad ahāny adya dve ca me niḥsṛtasya vai / puṣyeṇa saṃprayāto 'smi śravaṇe punarāgataḥ
It is forty-two days since I left. I set out under Puṣya and have returned under Śravaṇa.
Anuśāsana 13.153.27
aṣṭapañcāśataṃ rātryaḥ śayānasyādya me gatāḥ / śareṣu niśitāgreṣu yathā varṣaśataṃ tathā
Fifty-eight nights have passed as I lie on sharp arrows, each like a hundred years.
Anuśāsana 13.153.28
māgho 'yaṃ samanuprāpto māsaḥ puṇyo yudhiṣṭhira / tribhāgaśeṣaḥ pakṣo 'yaṃ śuklo bhavitum arhati
The holy month of Māgha has come, Yudhiṣṭhira. This should be the bright fortnight, with a third remaining.
Strī 11.25.41
tvam apy upasthite varṣe ṣaṭtriṃśe madhusūdana / hatajñātir hatāmātyo hataputro vanecaraḥ
You too, Madhusūdana, when the 36th year comes, will roam the forest, your kin, ministers and sons slain.
Mausala 16.1.1
ṣaṭtriṃśe tv atha samprāpte varṣe kauravanandanaḥ / dadarśa viparītāni nimittāni yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
When the 36th year arrived, Yudhiṣṭhira saw adverse omens.
